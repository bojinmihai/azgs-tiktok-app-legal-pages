AZGS local TikTok integration

AZGS local is an internal Windows workspace operated by AZ Grand Solutions for its authorized team. The TikTok integration is intended to let an authorized administrator view the firm's TikTok profile and public videos from the local application.

The integration is being configured in TikTok Sandbox. It is not a public sign-up service. This page does not mean TikTok has approved the application or that account authorization is complete.

What it can do

With account authorization, the connector reads basic profile information and a list of public videos, including video links and available engagement counts. The requested scopes are user.info.basic and video.list.

The current TikTok connector does not publish, schedule, edit or delete TikTok videos, read private messages, or run advertising campaigns. Other services connected to AZGS local are separate integrations.
