TikTok integration — terms of use

1. Operator and intended use

AZ Grand Solutions operates AZGS local for its internal business use. Contact: info@azgs.nl. Only people authorized by the operator may use the application or connect a TikTok account they own or are permitted to manage.

2. Authorization and functionality

Connecting an account requires authorization from its holder and the permissions allowed by TikTok. The current integration supports reading basic profile information and public video metadata. It does not support publishing, editing, deleting or scheduling TikTok content, private messaging or advertising. An app configuration or sandbox registration alone is not completed account authorization.

3. Responsible use

Use the integration only for authorized business purposes and in accordance with applicable law and TikTok's terms and developer policies. Do not share account passwords or access tokens in AI chats, public repositories or these public pages. Do not connect accounts belonging to others without their authorization, misrepresent fetched data, or attempt to bypass TikTok's access controls.

4. AI-assisted work

When an operator requests analysis by an AI participant, relevant retrieved TikTok content can be sent to that participant's configured AI provider. The operator must have authority to make this request and review the information before using or publishing any resulting draft. Generating a draft does not publish anything to TikTok.

5. Availability and control

The application runs on the operator's PC. API availability depends on internet access, valid authorization, TikTok permissions and quotas. Tokens can expire or be revoked. The integration may be unavailable or return incomplete results; no follower growth, reach or business result is guaranteed.

6. Disconnecting and deletion

The operator can disconnect TikTok in AZGS local and revoke the application's access through TikTok's account controls. Disconnecting removes locally stored connector credentials; it does not automatically erase existing chat history or saved analysis. Follow the privacy notice to request removal of retained data.

7. Changes and questions

These terms cover the functionality described on the date shown below. Changes to requested access or supported uses require an updated notice and, where required, renewed authorization. Contact info@azgs.nl with questions. These terms do not remove rights that cannot be waived under applicable law.
